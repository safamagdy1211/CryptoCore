WHAT IS THE RAIL FENCE CIPHER?

The Rail Fence cipher is a classical transposition cipher. Unlike substitution ciphers, the letters are not changed — they are only rearranged by writing them in a zigzag pattern across a set number of "rails" (rows).

HOW IT WORKS

Write the message diagonally downward and upward across n rails. Then read the rows left-to-right one by one to form the ciphertext. Decryption reconstructs the zigzag positions and fills letters back in.

Period = 2 × (rails − 1)
Rail of char i: |((i mod period) − (rails−1))| + (depends on bounce)

EXAMPLE — 3 RAILS

For HELLOWORLD with 3 rails:

Rail 0: H . . . O . . . D
Rail 1: . E . L . W . L .
Rail 2: . . L . . . R . .
─────────────────────────
Result: HOOD ELWL LR → HOODELWLLR

OUTPUT FREQUENCY

Letter distribution in ciphertext (same — transposition)
